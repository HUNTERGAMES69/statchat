Two-factor authentication

Once enabled, signing in asks for a six-digit code from your authenticator app as well as your password. Nothing else changes — entering plays, Undo and everything during a game work exactly as before. This protects against someone getting hold of your password; it is not a guard against your own mistakes.

Before you enable it, read this. After enrolling, sign out and sign back in to prove the code works — at a desk, not at a stadium. If the phone is ever lost, an admin can clear the factor from the Supabase SQL editor with delete from auth.mfa_factors where user_id = '<uuid>'; The SQL editor never asks for a code, so you cannot be locked out permanently. Enrol the second admin account too — it is your way back in.
