Three directions

Each shown at 40px, 28px and 18px, in a lockup, and on both backgrounds — because a mark that only works large and dark is not a logo. The current mark is a football with bars stuck on it; all three below drop the football silhouette, which is the part that reads as clip art.

1 · Hash marks

The marks painted down the middle of every field, which also read as a bar chart. Two true meanings in one shape, no football required. Scales down better than anything with an outline, and the gold bar gives it a focal point.

2 · The lower third

An abstracted scorebug: accent block, then two rows of different length. It is literally what the product puts on screen, and it is the only one of the three that says "broadcast" rather than "statistics". Reads as a monogram at small sizes.

3 · The down marker

The sideline down box, squared off: a numeral plate on a post. Instantly football to anyone who has stood on a sideline, and nothing to do with a ball. The plate can carry a number, which makes it flexible for a favicon or an app icon.
