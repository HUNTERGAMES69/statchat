Team

The full box,
both sides of it

Every figure below is kept for your team and the opponent, from the one play log your scorer is filling in. Nobody enters the other side separately.

Moving the ball

Offense

Rushing yards, passing yards and total yards. First downs, split by rush, pass and penalty. Third-down and fourth-down conversions, made and attempted. Explosive plays over twenty yards. Plays run, yards per play, and drives.

Stopping it

Defense and takeaways

Sacks and sack yardage. Interceptions, fumbles forced and fumbles recovered, and the turnover margin that falls out of them. Tackles for loss. Points allowed by quarter.

The other columns

Penalties, possession, situations

Penalties and penalty yards. Time of possession and number of possessions. Red-zone trips and what came of each. Points off turnovers. Scoring by quarter and by half.

Kicking game

Special teams

Punts, punt yards and net average. Kickoffs and touchbacks. Field goals and extra points, made and attempted, with distance. Kick and punt returns kept separately, with yards and the long.

Individual

Every player,
every unit

Per game and across the season. A player who takes a snap on offense, makes a tackle on defense and returns a kick appears in all three, because that is what he did.

Passing

Completions and attempts, completion percentage, yards, yards per attempt, longest completion, touchdowns, interceptions, and sacks taken with yards lost.

Rushing

Attempts, yards, yards per carry, longest run, touchdowns, and fumbles — separated into fumbles lost and fumbles recovered by your own side.

Receiving

Receptions, targets, catch rate, yards, yards per catch, longest reception, and touchdowns. Targets are the figure most stat sheets at this level simply do not keep.

Defense

Total tackles, split solo and assisted. Tackles for loss. Sacks. Interceptions with return yards. Forced fumbles and fumble recoveries. Passes defended.

Kicking and punting

Field goals made and attempted with the long, extra points, kickoffs and touchbacks. Punts, gross and net average, punts inside the twenty, and the long.

Returns

Kick returns and punt returns kept apart, each with attempts, yards, average, longest and touchdowns. A muffed catch recovered and run back is scored as what it was, not dropped.

Across the year

A season is not
ten box scores

Game stats are a by-product of scoring the game. The season view is the one a coach actually argues from, and it is built from the same play logs with nothing re-entered.

Season totals and averages

Every team and individual figure above, aggregated across the year, per game and per attempt. Leaders in each category, which is what the broadcast overlays read.

Trends, not just totals

Scoring and yardage by quarter and by half, across the season. Where the drives start. What the third-down rate does after halftime.

Opponent columns too

The same season shape for what has been done to you. A defense that is fine until the fourth quarter shows up here and nowhere else.

Deleted, preseason and sample games stay out

Season figures count games in the current season that are in progress or final. A game you deleted is not quietly still in the totals, a scrimmage marked preseason does not inflate the count, and the sample game every new account starts with is excluded. This is asserted in the test suite, because it is the kind of thing that goes wrong silently and makes every number on the page wrong at once.
