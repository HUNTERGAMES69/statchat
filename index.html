What it does

Built for the crew,
not the clipboard

Most stat software assumes the numbers are the point and the broadcast is somebody else's problem. If you are running a camera, a switcher and a commentator, that is backwards.

Overlays

Graphics that composite

Score bug, team stats, season leaders and a full play-by-play, each a browser input in vMix. No capture card, no key and fill, no desktop install. Add the URL and it is on your programme feed.

Entry

One pass, not two

A guided flow for kickoffs, punts, field goals and every ordinary snap. The scorer enters the play once; the overlays, the live spectator page and the box score all update from it.

Reports

NFHS, not NFL

Sack yardage charged to team rushing. Kneels and bad snaps as team rushes. No defensive score on a try. The conventions your state association actually uses, exported to PDF and Excel.

A game, start to finish

How a Friday runs

The order below is the order it happens in. Nothing here needs a second operator or a second machine.

01

Set up, midweek

Build the game, import the roster and pick your starters. Paste the opponent's colours in and every graphic takes them automatically.

02

Add the inputs

Point vMix at the overlay URLs once. They stay pointed. Going on air is a toggle in the app, not a change to your switcher.

03

Enter the game

Play by play, from a laptop or an iPad at the table. Undo works. So does a correction three drives later, without renumbering anything.

04

Hand it over

A single-game stat package or a full season report, as PDF or Excel, plus a public recap link a parent can open without an account.
