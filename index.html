What you get

The only high-school stat platform
that hands your crew a live dashboard

Most stat software treats the numbers as the point and the broadcast as somebody else's problem. If you are running a camera, a switcher and a commentator, that is backwards.

Crew View

A dedicated real-time dashboard — every play, every team and individual stat — that updates the instant a play is entered. Built for the broadcast team, by a broadcast team.

Plugs into your broadcast

Live JSON and XML drop straight into vMix, OBS, Wirecast, Livestream Studio, CasparCG or anything else that reads a URL. A ready-made transparent overlay is included for crews who would rather not build their own package.

Built for the pace of a game

Every play is three taps: pick the play type, fill in what happened, review before you save. No stats department, no training class — one person, the sideline, and a device they already own.

Stats

Nobody beats StatChat on stats

Everything below is in every account. There is no tier where the good numbers live.

Team

The full box, both sides

Rushing, passing and total yards. First downs. Third and fourth down conversion. Explosive plays over twenty yards. Sacks, interceptions, fumble recoveries. Penalties and penalty yards. Time of possession, possessions, plays per game. Kept for your team and the opponent, from the same play log.

Individual

Every player, every unit

Passing with completion percentage, yards per attempt, long, touchdowns, interceptions and sacks taken. Rushing and receiving with attempts, targets, catch rate and yards per touch. Tackles, tackles for loss and takeaways on defence. Kickoffs, punts, field goals and extra points on special teams. Kick and punt returns kept separately.

Trends

The season, not just the night

Yardage by game and by half. Run–pass balance by attempt and by yard. Points by quarter. Turnovers, sacks and penalties game over game. Third and fourth down rate plotted across the year. A season report a coach can actually hand to someone.

Out of the box

Take it with you

Single-game stat packages and full-season reports as PDF or Excel. A public recap link a parent can open without an account. Live JSON and XML for your graphics. Nothing locked in a format only StatChat can read.

Any device

It is a web page. Windows, macOS, iPad, Android tablet, a phone at a pinch — whatever is already in the booth or in your bag. Nothing to install, nothing to update between games, no capture card and no dongle.

No lock-in

One URL, set once

Your broadcast software points at a URL that never changes — no game id, no week number. Set the data sources up once in the pre-season and never touch them again. StatChat works out which game is live from the dashboard.

Built on NFHS rules, not adapted from someone else's

    
Automatic first down under NFHS belongs to four fouls, not the dozen the NFL awards
       it to. Pass interference is fifteen yards with no automatic first down, not the NFL's
       spot foul. StatChat gets these right because it was built for high-school football
       specifically — so the stat sheet you hand a coach matches the rule book the officials
       are using.
